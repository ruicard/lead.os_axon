# Performance Evaluation Guidance

Use these questions to prepare consistent, evidence-based observations for the five performance evaluation criteria in LeadOS.

## Efficiency

How consistently does the individual meet deadlines and deliver expected results? Can you provide specific examples of their ability to prioritize tasks effectively?

In what ways does their approach to tasks demonstrate rational implementation and operational efficiency?

**Challenge question:** When comparing two individuals, what differentiates the more efficient performer in terms of their output, speed, and prioritization skills?

## Quality

How would you describe the accuracy and thoroughness of the individual's work? How often are revisions or corrections needed?

How reliable is this person in upholding commitments to colleagues, stakeholders, and customers?

**Challenge question:** Can we quantify the impact of their work quality, or lack thereof, on the project, team, or customer?

## Engagement

How does the individual react to and handle changes in tasks or working conditions? Can you provide an example of their resilience?

To what extent do they demonstrate initiative by introducing new ideas or suggesting improvements?

**Challenge question:** Does the individual proactively seek solutions and drive change, or do they primarily react to assigned tasks?

## Responsibility-Taking

Does the individual take full ownership of their responsibilities and their outcomes, both positive and negative?

How effectively do they balance customer needs, cost-consciousness, and achieving their primary targets?

**Challenge question:** When a project faces difficulties, does this individual step up to help resolve the issue, even if it is outside their direct responsibility?

## Cooperation

How actively does the individual participate in collaborative tasks, share information, and contribute to team goals?

How do they manage disagreements or conflicts within the team or project?

**Challenge question:** Does this person elevate the performance of others through their cooperation, or do they primarily focus on their individual contributions?
