# Bosch Potential Indicators

## What is potential?

Potential is a person's ability to successfully apply and extend knowledge and
competences in new contexts. Dealing with the question of potential therefore
focuses on those qualities behind the competences that facilitate the process of
passing on and developing knowledge, experience and skill sets for future and
even more challenging tasks. Strengths of the potential indicators can give
hints, how far an associate has this potential.

## What are the Bosch potential indicators?

### Learning Ambition
The interest in continuing to develop oneself and one's abilities.

- **Curiosity** - In order to constantly understand and use complex and non-transparent circumstances and situations, one must have an intrinsic interest in new issues and in exploring uncharted territory.
- **Willingness to change** - In order to develop successful strategies in various different contexts, one needs to adapt one's own behavior patterns easily and with enthusiasm to given circumstances.
- **Self-reflection** - The awareness of one's own qualities, strength and weaknesses facilitates personal growth and learning from achievements and mistakes. Only this makes explicit and implicit feedback truly accessible and valuable.

### Emotional Power
The ability to remain able to work, perform and interact even in difficult situations.

- **Inner stability** - Associates in higher level must also be able to work, act and decide under pressure and in difficult situations without such pressure causing mental overload.
- **Courage** - The necessary readiness to exceed bounds, run risks and make mistakes when tackling problems and challenges of any dimension.
- **Empathy** - For interacting trustingly and constructively with people of own and foreign cultures, it is indispensable to be open-minded to the perspectives, qualities, thoughts and emotions of others.

### Interpersonal Impact
Impact on other people, on peers, on followers and in networks.

- **Networking** - Executives in large organizations need different and flexible possibilities of exerting influence. They must be able to establish networks far beyond their own company and use them for their particular topics and initiatives.
- **Assertiveness** - Interpersonal impact also means assertiveness. In fierce disputes with strong counterparts, a manager needs to be capable of asserting own issues constructively, firmly and effectively.
- **Creative drive** - People vary with regard to their creative drive. Working at high levels calls for power and endurance. Only clearly defined personal objectives and motivations make this possible in the long run.

### Intellectual Capacity
The ability to look at a situation and see the relevant correlations and influence possibilities.

- **Strategic perspective** - Facts and interrelations of strategy importance for the company must be clearly recognized, thoroughly understand and assessed as such.
- **Judgment** - Within short time, people must be able to make decisions of considerable impact and to defend them. This requires good sensors as well as decision-making strategies.
- **Managing complexity** - Coping with complex issues in a positive and conscious way calls for fundamental interest in group dynamic with all their hidden norms, contrasts and dilemmas.
